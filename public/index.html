It started with a question

A tiny bamboo whisk, a bowl of matcha and an idea. That was the whole idea behind Matcha Want? Exactly. Matcha Want is a friendly neighborhood matcha bar and cafe.

Our matcha is stone-ground from small farms in Uji and blended fresh each week. The rest, like our butter, milk, and even the yeast starters all come from local sellers within our community here in Orlando.
